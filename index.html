01BIENVENUE CHEZ NOUS

The story of Maison Lumière

A neighbourhood
French table.

Maison Lumière is a neighbourhood French restaurant built around the pleasure of gathering. Our room takes its cue from Parisian bistros: warm light, an unhurried table, and a welcome that makes every guest feel at home. Our team pairs thoughtful service with the rituals of French dining—a good apéritif, fresh bread to share, seasonal dishes inspired by regional classics, and time for one more conversation. Whether it’s a weekday supper or a special occasion, there’s always room at our table.
